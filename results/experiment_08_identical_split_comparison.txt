Experiment 8: Identical-Split Comparison

Model comparison:
1. TF-IDF (unigrams and bigrams) + Logistic Regression
2. DistilBERT layer-wise representations + Logistic Regression

Dataset:
500 sentences across 5 semantic categories.
100 sentences per category.

Method:
Both models were evaluated using the same five stratified 80/20 train-test splits with random seeds 0-4.

TF-IDF Results:
Split 1: 0.9800
Split 2: 0.9800
Split 3: 0.9500
Split 4: 0.9400
Split 5: 0.9700
Mean accuracy: 0.9640
Standard deviation: 0.0162

DistilBERT Results:
Layer 0: Mean accuracy = 0.9580, Standard deviation = 0.0147
Layer 1: Mean accuracy = 0.9500, Standard deviation = 0.0190
Layer 2: Mean accuracy = 0.9580, Standard deviation = 0.0160
Layer 3: Mean accuracy = 0.9560, Standard deviation = 0.0162
Layer 4: Mean accuracy = 0.9640, Standard deviation = 0.0196
Layer 5: Mean accuracy = 0.9640, Standard deviation = 0.0206
Layer 6: Mean accuracy = 0.9620, Standard deviation = 0.0194

Interpretation:
The identical-split comparison provides a direct comparison between lexical TF-IDF features and DistilBERT representations because both models are evaluated on exactly the same train-test splits.

Limitation:
The dataset is manually constructed and may contain category-specific lexical or structural cues. Therefore, the comparison should not be interpreted as a general comparison between TF-IDF and DistilBERT.
