
Experiment 7: TF-IDF Lexical Baseline

Model:
TF-IDF (unigrams and bigrams) with Logistic Regression.

Dataset:
500 sentences across 5 semantic categories.
100 sentences per category.

Method:
1. Used the existing 500-sentence dataset.
2. Split the data into 80% training and 20% test sets.
3. Used five stratified splits with random seeds 0–4.
4. Fitted the TF-IDF vectorizer only on each training set.
5. Trained Logistic Regression on the TF-IDF features.
6. Evaluated accuracy on each test set.
7. Calculated mean accuracy and standard deviation.

Results:

Split 1: 0.98
Split 2: 0.98
Split 3: 0.95
Split 4: 0.94
Split 5: 0.97

Mean accuracy: 0.964
Standard deviation: 0.01625

Observation:
The TF-IDF baseline achieved a mean classification accuracy of 96.4% across five stratified train-test splits.

Interpretation:
The high accuracy indicates that lexical features provide substantial information for distinguishing the predefined categories. The TF-IDF baseline's mean accuracy exceeded the mean probing accuracy observed in Experiment 6.

Limitation:
The dataset is manually constructed, and the experiment uses five train-test splits. The results do not establish that lexical features explain all information available in DistilBERT representations. A controlled comparison using identical splits is needed.
